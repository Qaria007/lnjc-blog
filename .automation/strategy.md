## The finding that drives everything

Yemeni distributors run brochure websites with no ongoing content, so the technical Arabic
library already puts LNJC ahead of the entire local field. But the commercially valuable
questions, how to register a product in Yemen, how to appoint a local representative, how to get
goods in, are currently answered by foreign regulatory consultancies who have never imported
anything into Yemen. LNJC is an actual licensed importer in Sana'a with signed exclusive
agreements. On those questions it can write what they cannot.

That reader, a manufacturer looking for a Yemen partner, is worth far more than a single local
order and is the exact relationship LNJC already has with Shivani Scientific and BOKANG BIO.

## Target mix

Roughly one technical Arabic article for every two English or commercial pieces. As of
2026-08-14 the split was 15 Arabic to 3 English, which is the imbalance to correct.

Progress check, 2026-09-10: the split is now 27 Arabic to 7 English. English is still badly
behind, because the queue below was worked from the top and the Arabic packs happened to be
ready first. The refilled queue is ordered two English to one Arabic and should be worked in
order, not cherry picked, or the gap will not close.

## Accuracy warning for the registration topics

Almost everything published online about SBDMA requirements comes from consultancies selling
registration services, not from the authority itself. Those pages contradict each other on
timelines and required documents. Before any pack is built for items 1 to 5, the specifics must
be traced to an official Yemeni source or to a document the authority itself issued. Where that
is not possible, the article must describe LNJC's own direct experience as experience, plainly,
rather than stating it as a regulatory requirement. This is the same failure mode recorded in
`.automation/README.md`.

## Generative engine optimisation

Buyers increasingly get an answer from an AI assistant instead of a results page. Those systems
favour pages that answer one question, state facts plainly, and attribute each to a named
source. The house style already does this. Maintain it, keep `llms.txt` current in English as
articles publish, and keep stating concretely what LNJC is licensed to do and which
manufacturers it represents.
