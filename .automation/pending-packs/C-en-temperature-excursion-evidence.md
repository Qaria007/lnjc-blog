## Why this topic

`ar/temperature-excursion.html` covers this for the Arabic technical reader. Nothing covers it for
the manufacturer sitting outside the country who has just been told a shipment went out of range
and has to decide whether to believe the explanation. That reader wants to know what a competent
distributor should be able to produce, and how quickly.

## IMPORTANT, READ BEFORE WRITING

Aimed at manufacturers outside Yemen. Do not state Yemen specific regulatory requirements of any
kind. Explain the international framework, then be plain that local specifics are confirmed case by
case, and invite the reader to ask LNJC.

Specific to this topic: do not state numeric temperature limits, excursion durations, or stability
budgets unless the verified text in this pack states them. Ranges such as 2 to 8 degrees appear in
the sources and may be quoted where they do, but never extend them into a claim about how long a
given product tolerates an excursion. That is a product specific question that belongs to the
manufacturer's own stability data, and saying otherwise on a public page could get a real batch
released that should not be.
