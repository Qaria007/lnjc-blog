## Why this topic

This is the single most useful thing LNJC can say to its highest value reader. A manufacturer
choosing a partner in an unfamiliar market has no way to tell a real distributor from a letterhead,
and the international guidelines already describe what a competent one looks like. Writing that
down plainly, then inviting the reader to apply the same checklist to LNJC, is a stronger argument
than any claim LNJC could make about itself.

## IMPORTANT, READ BEFORE WRITING

This article is aimed at manufacturers outside Yemen who are looking for a distribution partner.
You will be tempted to state Yemen specific regulatory requirements: how long SBDMA registration
takes, exactly which documents it wants, what it charges. DO NOT. The Yemeni authority's own
website is unreachable, and almost everything published on this subject online comes from
consultancies selling registration services, who contradict each other. Nothing in this pack is a
Yemen specific regulatory requirement and you may not present anything as one.

Explain the general international framework accurately from the verified sources, then be plain
that the exact requirements in Yemen are confirmed case by case with the authority, and invite the
reader to ask LNJC, a licensed importer that does this, rather than guessing. Write the closing
invitation as a genuine offer to answer questions, not as a sales pitch.

A second trap specific to this topic: do not turn the checklist into a description of LNJC. Write
the criteria neutrally, as things any manufacturer should ask any candidate distributor, and let
the reader draw the conclusion.
