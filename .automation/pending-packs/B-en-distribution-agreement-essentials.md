## IMPORTANT, READ BEFORE WRITING

Two separate hazards here, and they need different handling.

First, the usual one. This article is aimed at manufacturers outside Yemen looking for a
distribution partner. Do not state Yemen specific regulatory requirements: not registration
timelines, not the document list, not fees. The authority's own website is unreachable and the
consultancy pages that fill the gap contradict each other. Explain the international framework,
then say plainly that the specifics are confirmed case by case, and invite the reader to ask.

Second, and particular to this topic. Most of what belongs in a distribution agreement is
commercial practice, not regulation: territory, term, minimum purchase, pricing, stock cover,
termination, what happens to registrations if the parties separate. The guidelines will support
some of it, mainly the quality and responsibility clauses, and will say nothing about the rest.
Where the sources are silent, write it plainly as common commercial practice with no document
attached to it. Do not dress a commercial norm up as a regulatory requirement. That is the same
failure mode as the recall incident in `.automation/README.md`, just in a different costume.

You are also not a lawyer and the article must not read as legal advice. Say once, plainly, that
the agreement should be reviewed by someone qualified in the relevant jurisdictions.

## CANDIDATE SOURCES, NOT YET VERIFIED

1. European Commission, Guidelines of 5 November 2013 on Good Distribution Practice
   (2013/C 343/01). URL known good as of 2026-08-14.
   URL: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX:52013XC1123(01)
   Extract: Chapter 7 on outsourced activities, which is the closest thing in the guidelines to a
   contract specification, covering what the contract giver and contract acceptor each owe. Also
   Chapter 5 on qualification of suppliers and customers, and Chapter 6 on complaints, returns and
   recalls, since responsibility for those has to be allocated somewhere in the agreement.
