## Why this topic

The Arabic library covers the distributor's own premises, records, staff and procedures well, but
not the two doors: who the distributor is allowed to buy from, and who it is allowed to sell to.
That is a distinct requirement in the guidelines and it is the control that keeps falsified stock
out of a legitimate chain. `ar/falsified-and-substandard-medicines.html` covers the products
themselves, not the counterparty checks, so this does not overlap.
