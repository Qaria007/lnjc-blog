# Directory listings for LNJC: where to list, and exactly what to paste

Prepared 2026-08-15. Every web address below was checked on that date and responded.

## Why this matters

Google currently reports the blog as "Discovered, currently not indexed". That is Google saying
it knows the site exists but does not yet consider it worth crawling. The usual cause is that
almost nothing on the internet points at the company. The main site now links to the blog, which
was the biggest gap. Directory listings are the next layer: each one is a mention of the company
name, address and phone in a place search engines already trust, and several of them are also
where Yemeni buyers and foreign manufacturers actually look.

## THE ONE RULE: paste the details identically every time

Search engines match businesses across the web by comparing name, address and phone. If the
address is written three different ways, they may be treated as three different companies and
the benefit is lost. Copy the blocks below character for character. Do not improvise, do not
abbreviate, do not translate the address differently each time.

against you. Keep the full wording even when a form is tight on space; cut something else instead.

## Watch out for

**Paid upgrades.** Nearly all of these will offer a "premium", "verified" or "gold supplier"
package immediately after you submit the free listing. The free listing carries the search
benefit. Do not pay for any of them at this stage. If one of them starts sending you real
enquiries after a few months, reconsider then, with evidence.

**Directories that only want your money.** If a site has no real traffic, no Yemeni companies
already listed, and asks for payment before publishing anything, skip it. It will do nothing.

**Lead resellers.** Sites like aeroleads, zoominfo and similar sell contact data. They may already
list LNJC without asking. You do not submit to those, and there is little value in trying.

**Do not invent detail to fill a form.** Employee counts, revenue, year founded, certifications.
If you do not know it from your own records, leave the field empty. An empty field costs nothing;
a wrong one is repeated across the internet and is very hard to correct later.
