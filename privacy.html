Privacy policy

By LNJC· 6 August 2026

This policy covers landcarenj.com and blog.landcarenj.com, both operated by LNJC Pharmaceuticals and Medical Supplies Co., Ltd. It sets out, in plain terms, what information we collect from visitors and how we use it.

What we collect

We collect only what a visitor provides directly: a name, email address or phone number submitted through a contact form, a newsletter signup, or a message sent to us by email or phone. We do not require an account to read this blog or the main site, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

How we use it

Contact and newsletter details are used to reply to enquiries, to send occasional updates about LNJC's products and services, and to maintain our own business records. We do not sell or rent visitor information to third parties.

Fonts and third-party services

This site loads the Cairo and Tajawal typefaces from Google Fonts. Loading a font from Google's servers can expose a visitor's IP address to Google under Google's own privacy terms. We do not run advertising trackers or third-party analytics scripts on this blog.

Facebook

LNJC maintains a Facebook Page for company updates. Any interaction with that Page, including comments and messages, is additionally governed by Facebook's own privacy policy and terms, which we do not control.

Data retention and security

We keep enquiry and newsletter records only as long as needed for the purpose they were collected for, and take reasonable steps to protect them from unauthorised access. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You can ask us to stop contacting you, to tell you what information we hold about you, or to delete it, at any time by writing to us. We will act on a reasonable request without undue delay.

Changes to this policy

We may update this page as our practices or the sites change. The date above shows when it was last revised.
